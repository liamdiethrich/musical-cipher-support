Musical Cipher Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026

Native iOS app

Musical Cipher does not collect personal data, use advertising identifiers, track you, or send gameplay data off your device. Solved levels, listening preferences, and practice totals are stored locally so the app can resume your case. You can erase them with Reset all case progress in Settings; deleting the app also removes its local data.

The app does not request access to the microphone, camera, contacts, photos, location, health data, or Bluetooth. Interval audio is generated and played on device. The app has no accounts, advertising, purchases, social features, or free-text communication.

Desktop web game

The web game stores case progress in your browser’s local storage. Its built-in event records remain local in this release and reject raw audio, sensitive identifiers, and personal contact fields.

Children’s privacy

Musical Cipher does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Because the app has no account system or server-side gameplay collection, no child profile is created or transmitted.

Changes and contact

If a future release changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before release.
